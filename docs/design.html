$ Elevation & Depth

Never blurred. Light mode: ink borders + hard 3px key shadows on pressables, flat hairline cards. Dark mode: shadows and ink outlines disappear — depth comes from surface contrast and hairlines. Toggle the theme to watch the key shadow vanish.

$ Do's and Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Keep all UI copy in the terminal voice — $ prompts, file paths, flags — and keep it consistent.
  • Use the green strictly for interactive or "live" things: buttons, links, prompts, current status.
  • Light mode: ink border + hard offset shadow with hover-translate on every pressable. Dark mode: flat controls, background shifts instead.
  • Verify every new text/background pair at ≥ 4.5:1 in both modes before shipping (3:1 is the floor for large text and UI graphics only).
  • Reserve terminal dark islands for at most two per page in light mode.
  • Let monospace body text breathe: 1.7 line-height, ~60ch max measure.

✗ Don't

  • No gradients, blurred shadows, or glassmorphism — depth is borders, hard offsets (light), surface contrast (dark).
  • Never use pure #FFFFFF or #000000 as a surface; the only pure white is dark-mode button text, which needs it for contrast.
  • Don't add a second accent hue; traffic-light colors stay decorative or semantic.
  • Don't set body copy in Space Grotesk, or headings in mono at large sizes.
  • Don't let dark mode go neon — the greens stay garden, not phosphor.
  • Don't ship a card without its header, or a light-mode button without its shadow — half-applied treatments break the illusion.
